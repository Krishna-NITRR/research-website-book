A System-Driven Research Guide

A practical operator's guide to entering research, landing internships, and publishing early. Not a motivation book.

The barrier to research is not talent. It is the absence of a system. Most students who fail to enter research do not lack intelligence. They lack the right framework at the right time.

This book is a practical operator's guide. Each chapter is a working system you can execute, not a theory you have to interpret.
